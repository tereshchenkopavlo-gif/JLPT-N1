## Card-specific reasoning

- 759: 発見された explicitly states that the documents were discovered; the translation must retain the finding event.
- 761: 三対二 uses 対 as a ratio marker, separate from the つい pair sense.
- 766: 覚悟 indicates resolve; 新たな扉を開く is a metaphor for opening a new path/opportunity.
- 771: the Japanese definition includes 流れ; the Ukrainian gloss should include current/flow as well as tides.
- 772: 票 can be a counted vote, ballot, or ticket/slip depending on context; the current gloss duplicated “vote/ballot”.
- 774: 田んぼ does not itself say the fields are flooded at the time.
- 775: 日夜 contrasts day and night; “день у день” loses that contrast.
- 779: the definition should cover the building unit/wing as well as roof ridge and the counter.
- 780: the usage already includes 体格; the Japanese definition should not omit that sense.
- 785: 麻 refers to a group of fiber plants and the fibers/textiles made from them; the Ukrainian example translation should not insert a parenthetical material list.

No external dictionary claim is made for 蔵, 対, 扉, 潮, 票, 苗, 頂, 棟 or 柄; those edits align the exact Japanese sentence with its Ukrainian translation and the card's own usage note.
