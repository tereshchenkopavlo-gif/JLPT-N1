## Card-specific reasoning

- 857: 沿岸 can cover the region along the shore of a sea, lake or river; it does not specify an exact width.
- 858: 沿線 includes areas along railways and roads; the second example explicitly concerns the railway line.
- 860: the Buddhist sense of 縁起 is dependent arising; 吉兆 is only an auspicious omen, while 縁起 can cover good or bad omens.
- 862: 縁談が持ち込まれる means a marriage proposal/arrangement is brought forward, often through an introduction; the intermediary is contextual, not obligatory.
- 863: 診療 is broader than “treatment” alone; 整えられている describes a system already in place.
- 865: 汚職事件が発覚し／批判が集まっている describes corruption being exposed and public criticism continuing.
- 867: ひったくり is snatch theft; the Ukrainian translation should retain the act of belongings being snatched, without adding practical detail. The existing usage note already clarifies that 往来 is ongoing movement/exchange, not necessarily one completed round trip.
- 872: the Japanese definition now covers gold, golden colour and figurative value. The two example readings are context-specific editorial choices; the example sentences themselves remain unchanged.
- 873: 屋敷 can refer to the grounds/plot as well as the substantial house with grounds.
- 874: 運命に翻弄される is naturally rendered with the Ukrainian idiom “стати іграшкою долі”.
- 878: 下心 is a concealed ulterior motive, not necessarily one hidden behind outward friendliness. A personal or romantic interest can be one context, not the whole definition.
- 880: 塗装の前に壁面の下地を処理する means preparing the wall surface before painting.
