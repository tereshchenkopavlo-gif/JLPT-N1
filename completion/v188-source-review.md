- [Kotobank — 沖](https://kotobank.jp/word/%E6%B2%96-451423): the entry explicitly covers sea or lake, away from shore.
- [Kotobank — 核](https://kotobank.jp/word/%E6%A0%B8-43514) and [原子核](https://kotobank.jp/word/%E5%8E%9F%E5%AD%90%E6%A0%B8-60550): distinguish the figurative core, cell nucleus and atomic nucleus; 原子核 is the central part of an atom.
- [Kotobank — 幹](https://kotobank.jp/word/%E5%B9%B9-467141): defines 幹 as the tree trunk and as the central part of a matter; the encyclopedia describes it as the thick, upright main axis from which branches grow.
- [Kotobank — 管渠](https://kotobank.jp/word/%E7%AE%A1%E6%B8%A0-236525) and [配水管](https://kotobank.jp/word/%E9%85%8D%E6%B0%B4%E7%AE%A1-598889): show 管 as くだ in isolation and かん in a compound such as 配水管.

## Card-specific linguistic reasoning

- 683: てもらった reports receiving the action; it does not entail that the subject requested it.
- 685: デザインする covers the design of the store, not only decoration.
- 691: 大切に身に着ける combines valuing the item and wearing it; 職人 explicitly identifies a craftsperson.
- 697: the general noun 闇 does not always mean “black market”; 闇取引 is the compound that denotes illicit/hidden trade.
- 707: the usage note explicitly distinguishes standalone 管（くだ） from かん in compounds, while retaining 管を巻く（くだをまく）.
- 708: 組織の癌 is a strongly negative figurative label for a destructive obstacle; Japanese does not encode duration, so “задавнена” was removed.

No external source is claimed for the Ukrainian stylistic choices in 683, 685, 691, 697 or 708; those decisions follow the sentence grammar and the current card's own usage context.
