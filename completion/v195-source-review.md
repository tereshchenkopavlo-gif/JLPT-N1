## Card-specific reasoning

- 911: the pilot suggested replacing “надприродній істоті” with “надприродну істоту”, but the current phrase is “акцентує на надприродній істоті”; the preposition “на” requires the locative here, so the current wording is grammatical and was accepted unchanged.
- 914: 改築 is rebuilding all or part of a building; the general definition does not require an explicit change to structure or layout.
- 915: the two verbs 改訂 and 見直す occur in the same Japanese sentence; the Ukrainian translation should avoid repeating “перегляд”.
- 920: 開催 means holding/conducting an event; 主催 identifies the organizer.
- 922: 開幕 covers the start/opening of an event and the figurative beginning of a new era.
- 927: 重視される is naturally rendered as considering the evaluation important / giving weight to evaluation, not the calque “give importance to evaluating”.
- 928: 無断 in the family context can be “without notice”; “without permission” would assume a permission relation not explicit in the sentence.
- 932: 概要 is an outline/overview; the Ukrainian sentence should read as a natural short briefing on the project.
- 933: 概略をつかむ means grasp the overall picture; it is not an adverbial use meaning “approximately”.
- 934: 会社員 means company employee; “службовець” can imply public service in Ukrainian.
- 936: 事業者 is broader than an individual entrepreneur; “суб’єкт господарювання” covers business operators/entities.
- 941: the original definition mixed two constructions: 格闘する can take と, but 取り組む takes に. The corrected definition uses 困難な問題に取り組み.

No Japanese example, ID, POS, quiz target, comparison pair or progress-storage logic is changed.
