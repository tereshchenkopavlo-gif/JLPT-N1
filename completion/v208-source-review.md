## Source-specific notes

- **実刑 (1380):** Kotobank, デジタル大辞泉 and 精選版 日本国語大辞典 define 実刑 as a sentence actually served without 執行猶予. The Ukrainian gloss and example are aligned to actual imprisonment, not any generic conviction or punishment.
- **嫉妬 (1378):** the existing card usage already distinguishes envy of another's success from romantic jealousy; the Japanese definition is expanded so both senses are represented consistently.
- **手数 (1405):** the card's own usage and pilot distinguish てすう (trouble/effort) from てかず (number of moves, especially in board games); the note is reorganized by sense.

No new unverified source is introduced. The pilot hashes are retained as provenance.
