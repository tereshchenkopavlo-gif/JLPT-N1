## Scope limits

- The POS map is a learning label and pool key; it is not a claim that every possible dictionary sense is taught in the card.
- ID3565 is deliberately ナ形 for the selected timely sense; the separate baseball noun sense is not imported into the card.
- ID3636 retains a documented source-confidence limitation; no stronger dictionary claim is made.
- All 12 IDs exceed the documented v153 pilot range 121–320. Pilot credit = 0.
