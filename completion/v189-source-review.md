- [Kotobank — 股](https://kotobank.jp/word/%E8%82%A1-493415) and [二股](https://kotobank.jp/word/%E4%BA%8C%E8%82%A1-617390): support the anatomical and branching/fork senses.
- [Kotobank — 柵](https://kotobank.jp/word/%E6%9F%B5-68672): describes a fence as a partition made from separate elements; it need not be a continuous wall.
- [Kotobank — 主](https://kotobank.jp/word/%E4%B8%BB-76657): includes owner/master and central or principal person.
- [Kotobank — 趣き](https://kotobank.jp/word/%E8%B6%A3%E3%81%8D-3137002): supports both atmosphere/appeal and the gist or content of a matter.
- [Kotobank — 沼](https://kotobank.jp/word/%E6%B2%BC-111031): gives the shallow-water sense and a colloquial figurative use for an object of deep absorption.
- [Kotobank — 証拠](https://kotobank.jp/word/%E8%A8%BC%E6%8B%A0-529127) and [信証](https://kotobank.jp/word/%E4%BF%A1%E8%A8%BC-537590): connect 証 with grounds, evidence and a sign/witness.

## Card-specific reasoning

- 712: the two examples explicitly use 逆 for an opposite result and an opposite standpoint, not only order/direction.
- 720: the usage field already documents 一件・二件; the Japanese definition should not omit that productive counter use.
- 724: the second example is about removing accumulated old practices; “moral impurity” overstates and misclassifies that figurative sense.
- 739: the second example describes a person treated like the company’s master/owner, not merely a long-time resident.
- 741: the literary “gist/content” sense is distinct from the atmosphere or character of a room.
- 744: 規則を盾にする is using a rule as cover or justification, not receiving patronage.
- 746: the examples use 証 as a symbolic sign of a bond and of a life, not only as factual evidence.
- 749: 誠に申し訳ございませんでした contains no stated cause; the translation must not invent one.

No separate external dictionary claim is made for 逆, 件, 垢, 盾 or 誠; these edits align the current definition/usage with the examples and pilot notes.
