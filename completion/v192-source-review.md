## Card-specific reasoning

- 815: 圧倒する技術力 compares the company's technological capability with competitors' capability, not with the competitors as people.
- 818: 主に marks political purpose as typical rather than mandatory; 暗殺未遂 remains distinct from a completed 暗殺.
- 819: 暗算 is mental calculation; “oral arithmetic” can incorrectly suggest that numbers must be spoken aloud.
- 822: 第三者機関 means a third-party/outside organization; independence is not asserted unless separately established.
- 825: 確認し合った conveys mutual reaffirmation, not first-time discovery.
- 828: 異動 and 移動 are distinct homophones, not alternate spellings.
- 831: 遺言に is naturally rendered as “in the will”; the card's two readings are retained.
- 839: 一段落 marks a stage or phase coming to a pause/completion, not necessarily the whole undertaking.
- 844: replace the tautology 一連なり with an independent definition based on interrelated things forming a sequence.
- 845: 丹精込めて作り上げた conveys careful, painstaking craftsmanship.
- 855: remove the cumbersome nominal stack “access to viewing”; translate 閲覧 directly as viewing/consulting materials.
- 856: 人を援助する takes a direct object; for a recipient marked by に, use 人に援助を行う／与える.
