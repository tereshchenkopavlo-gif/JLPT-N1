## Card-specific reasoning

- 943: the second example should retain the metaphor of a large target/prey and the intention not to lose it, but “узявся до переговорів” is less natural than “розпочав переговори”.
- 944: 確信するに至った is naturally “остаточно переконався”; the conclusion follows from the investigation.
- 949: 学問 is broader than modern “science” alone and can mean scholarship/academic learning. 学芸員 is a museum curator, not a generic research employee.
- 951: 事態の収束 can be the settling/resolution of a situation; “crisis” is not explicit in the Japanese.
- 952: 表現に深みが出ない concerns expressive depth in the performance; avoid the calque “надати вираженню глибини”.
- 956: 葛藤 explicitly includes opposition/conflict between people or groups as well as internal conflict.
- 960: 勘定 includes counting quantities as well as calculating and paying an amount.
- 961: 勘弁してもらいたい is a colloquial request to be spared unreasonable demands; the literal “позбавили вимог” is unnatural.
- 963: 連載 is a work published/serialized in installments; “серіальна публікація” is a calque. “Цілісне ціле” is tautological.
- 965: 最新 means the latest, not merely modern.
- 968: 経営方針 is the company’s management/operating policy and priorities, not an abstract “course of management”.
- 969: よい感触を得た in negotiations can mean receiving an encouraging signal/response, not only forming a personal impression.

No Japanese example, POS, ID, quiz target, comparison pair or progress-storage logic is changed.
