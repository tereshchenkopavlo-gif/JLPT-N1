## Source-specific notes

- **指紋 (1334):** Kotobank, デジタル大辞泉 and 精選版 日本国語大辞典 define the word as the ridge pattern on the inner fingertip and also the image left by pressing it onto a surface. The Japanese definition and Ukrainian gloss distinguish those two senses.
- **施行 (1339):** 毎日ことばplus (2021-11-09) reports しこう as the standard reading for 施行, while せこう is a conventional reading in some administrative contexts; it distinguishes 施工（せこう）, the carrying out of construction work. The card retains both readings and explains context.
