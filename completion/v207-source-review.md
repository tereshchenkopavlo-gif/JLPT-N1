## Source-specific notes

- **自首 (1363):** Japanese Penal Code Article 42 states that a person who voluntarily surrenders before the investigative authority discovers the offense may receive discretionary sentence mitigation. The relevant timing is discovery of the criminal fact, not merely whether the offender's identity has been identified. Checked against the official e-Gov law text and a current article-by-article transcription/explanation. This is a lexical-definition correction, not legal advice.
- **磁気 (1359):** Hokkaido Electric Power Network explains that a magnetic field arises around magnets and current-carrying objects. The Japanese definition is broadened to cover the phenomenon associated with electric current, not only attraction of iron by a permanent magnet.
- **児童 (1354):** unchanged. The card's Japanese definition explicitly adds the broad sense 子ども; the demographic example does not require the narrower meaning “school pupils”.

No Japanese example changed. POS, quiz, comparison and progress-storage behavior are unchanged.
